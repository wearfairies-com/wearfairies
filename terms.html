What Fairies Is

Fairies is a peer-to-peer dress lending platform based in Boston, Massachusetts. We connect people who want to rent clothing (Renters) with people who want to lend their clothing (Lenders). Fairies acts as the intermediary — handling all logistics, delivery, collection, and payment processing.

Fairies is not a retail store. We do not own the clothing listed on the platform. All pieces belong to individual Lenders.

For Renters

Booking & Payment

  • All rentals must be booked in advance through the Fairies platform or by contacting us directly.
  • Payment is required to confirm your booking. Until payment is received, your booking is not guaranteed.
  • Prices are set by individual Lenders and displayed on each listing.
  • A delivery fee may apply depending on your location. This will be communicated before you confirm.

Delivery & Collection

  • We deliver to your door the day before or the morning of your event date.
  • We collect from your door the day after your event date.
  • You must be available or arrange for someone to receive and return the item.
  • If collection is missed, a $20 per day late fee applies until the item is collected.

Cancellations

  • Cancellations made more than 48 hours before delivery receive a full refund.
  • Cancellations made within 48 hours of delivery are non-refundable.
  • If Fairies cancels your booking for any reason, you will receive a full refund.

For Lenders

Listing Your Pieces

  • By listing a piece on Fairies you confirm that you own it and have the right to lend it.
  • All items must be clean, in good condition, and accurately described.
  • Fairies reserves the right to decline or remove any listing at our discretion.
  • You agree to make your piece available on the dates confirmed at booking.

Earnings

  • You earn a percentage of each rental fee. Your exact split will be agreed with Fairies before your listing goes live.
  • Earnings are paid out after each successful rental is completed.
  • Fairies retains a service fee from each transaction to cover delivery, platform costs, and operations.

We handle every piece with care. We expect Renters to do the same. This policy exists to protect Lenders — the real people whose clothing you're borrowing.

What Counts as Damage

  • Stains — any staining that cannot be removed by standard dry cleaning
  • Tears or rips — any tearing of fabric, seams, or embellishments
  • Stretching or distortion — any permanent change to the shape or fit of the item
  • Burns or scorch marks
  • Missing components — buttons, belts, embellishments, or accessories that are part of the listing
  • Loss — failure to return the item at all

What Does NOT Count as Damage

  • Minor creasing or wrinkling from wear
  • Light scent from normal wear (we steam all items before relisting)
  • Pre-existing condition documented before the rental

How Damage is Assessed

Every item is inspected by Fairies before delivery and again upon collection. We photograph all items before dispatch as a record of their condition.

If damage is identified on collection, the Renter will be notified within 24 hours with photographic evidence.

Damage Charges

Minor damage (stains removable by dry cleaning, minor repairs): cost of professional cleaning or repair, minimum $30

Major damage (irreparable tears, permanent staining, severe distortion): full replacement value of the item as listed by the Lender

Loss (item not returned): full replacement value plus a $50 administrative fee

Fairies retains its service fee regardless of damage outcome. The remaining charge goes directly to the Lender.

Disputes

If a Renter disputes a damage assessment, they may contact Fairies within 48 hours of being notified. We will review the photographic evidence and make a final decision. Our decision is final.
