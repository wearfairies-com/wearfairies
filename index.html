A note from the founder

Why I started WearFairies

Every woman I know has a dress she wore once and never again. And every one of us has had a night we wanted to feel extraordinary for, and couldn't justify spending hundreds on something we'd wear a single time.

WF is my answer to both. Real closets, real women in this city, and someone who personally carries every dress from one door to the next.
