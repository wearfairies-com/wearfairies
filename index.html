✦ What Is Fairies ✦

Every occasion deserves the right dress.
Not the full price tag.

Fairies is a peer-to-peer dress lending platform based in Boston. Real people share pieces from their wardrobes. Real people borrow them for their moments. We handle every pickup and delivery personally — door to door — so nobody has to figure out the logistics. You just find the dress and show up.

✦ About Fairies ✦

We started with a simple idea.

Every woman has a dress she wore once and never again. And every woman has had a moment — a graduation, a birthday, a date — where she wanted to look extraordinary but couldn't justify spending hundreds of dollars on something she'd wear for one night.

Fairies was built to close that gap. Not with a warehouse or a subscription. With real people, real wardrobes, and a personal service that handles every single logistic so you never have to think about anything except showing up.

We're based in Boston. We personally collect every piece from the lender's door and deliver it to the renter's door. We're building something that feels human in an industry that forgot to be.
